TRAINING SUMMARY - verify against the official source.

The EU Artificial Intelligence Act is a horizontal regulation that applies across sectors. Medical devices that contain an AI system and that need a notified body conformity assessment under the medical device regulations are, as a rule, treated as high-risk AI systems. The obligations for high-risk systems are phased in over several years after the Act entered into force.

Providers of high-risk AI systems must operate a risk management system for the AI, apply data governance to training, validation and test data, prepare technical documentation, enable automatic logging, give users clear information, design for human oversight, and ensure appropriate accuracy, robustness and cybersecurity. The intention is that these requirements are assessed together with the medical device conformity assessment rather than in a separate procedure.

What this means for a company: make an inventory of every product feature that uses AI or machine learning and record whether it falls in scope. Map each AI Act requirement to the existing MDR technical documentation to find gaps rather than creating a parallel file. Watch for harmonised standards and guidance, because they will define what "good enough" looks like for the evidence.
