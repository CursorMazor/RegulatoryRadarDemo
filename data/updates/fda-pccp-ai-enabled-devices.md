TRAINING SUMMARY - verify against the official source.

A predetermined change control plan (PCCP) lets a manufacturer describe, in the original marketing submission, the changes it intends to make to an AI-enabled device software function over time, together with how those changes will be developed, validated and rolled out. If the FDA agrees to the plan, changes that stay within it can be implemented without a new premarket submission.

A PCCP is usually made of three parts: a description of the planned modifications, a modification protocol that explains the data, training, testing and acceptance criteria that will be used, and an impact assessment that explains the benefits and risks of the changes and how they will be controlled.

What this means for a company: products that use machine learning and are expected to be retrained or tuned after launch are the natural candidates. Preparing a PCCP requires mature data management, clear performance metrics, a plan for detecting performance drift in the field, and labeling that tells users what may change. Teams should decide early in development whether a PCCP is worth including, because it shapes the validation evidence that has to be collected.
